* Lix Governance
The Lix project has published a [[https://wiki.lix.systems/books/lix-organisation/page/lix-governance][new page detailing their governance model]]. This
seems like a great way forward to formalize how the project works.

* Misc
** Fetch from savannah
[[https://github.com/normalcea][@normalcea]] opened a new [[https://github.com/NixOS/nixpkgs/pull/435964][PR in nixpkgs]] that deprecates =fetchFromSavannah=.

** nix-manipulator
[[https://github.com/hoh][@hoh]] [[https://discourse.nixos.org/t/announcing-nix-manipulator-nima-structured-edits-for-nix-that-keep-formatting/68513][announced a new project]], nix-manipulator, a python library and CLI to parse
and modify Nix code files. You can find it on [[https://github.com/hoh/nix-manipulator][GitHub]].

** Karl Voit's post about their issues with NixOS
Karl [[https://karl-voit.at/2025/08/30/end-of-my-nixos/][wrote a blog post]] about their experience with NixOS and the things they
didn't like about it and the parts of NixOS that failed them. I don't share most
of their views, but I think it's a good idea to learn what doesn't work for
other people and see if we can fix it.
